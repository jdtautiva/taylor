Board 01 · Policy framework

Dynamic Central Bank Simulator

Use this framework to diagnose current conditions, calculate a benchmark interest rate, and justify a monetary policy decision.

Taylor Rule · Complete Formula

it = rP+ πt+ θπ(πt − πt*)+ θy(Yt − Yt*)+ e(i,t)

The complete formula is also available on the tracking board.

Core Definitions

  • Inflation (πt)
  • Target (πt*)
  • Output Gap (Yt − Yt*)
  • Natural Rate (rP)

Decision Algorithm

  1. Diagnose (πt, gap)
  2. Compute it
  3. Interpret
  4. Decide

Policy Logic

Inflation ↑ and gap ↑ → Contract

Inflation ↓ and gap ↓ → Expand

Supply shock → Caution

Transmission

it ↑ → Demand ↓ → Gap ↓ → Inflation ↓

it ↓ → Demand ↑ → Gap ↑ → Inflation ↑

Key Takeaways

  • Contractionary policy stabilizes inflation when πt > πt*.
  • Expansionary policy supports recovery when output is below potential.
  • Caution is required under supply shocks.
  • Policy operates with lags → avoid overreaction.
